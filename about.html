ABOUT SARAL VIBES

Saral Vibes is a family-run saree business dedicated to bringing beautiful sarees to our customers. We celebrate the beauty of Indian tradition through elegant sarees for every occasion.

Our Story

Saral Vibes was born from a simple dream — to bring the timeless beauty of authentic Banarasi sarees to every corner of India. As two working engineers, we wanted to build something of our own while staying connected to the rich tradition of Banaras.

Our journey began with selling sarees through stalls, where we got the opportunity to share the beauty of Banarasi sarees with people. Sourced directly through our trusted retailer in Banaras, our collection carries the charm and tradition of this beautiful city. What started as a small initiative has now grown into a dream of making these sarees accessible to customers across India.
